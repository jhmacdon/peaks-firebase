Description

The Swiss Glacier Inventory 2023 provides glacier outlines (areas), debris cover, ice divides and location points of all glaciers in Switzerland referring to the years 2021-2024. The data is based on aerial imagery and stereophotogramatic analysis of the Federal office of Topography, Switzerland (swisstopo) within the framework of the Topographic landscape Model of Switzerland (swissTLM3D). Furthermore the dataset has been revised by Glaciologists of VAW-ETH Zurich, University of Zurich and University of Fribourg (Switzerland). Please find more details about this dataset in the publications listet below.
