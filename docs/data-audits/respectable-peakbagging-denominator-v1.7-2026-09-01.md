## What remains

The lists have 1,211 memberships across 723 source identities. Prior DoBIH
work reviewed 323 of those identities; 400 are new. No list may go live until
all identities, credited destination covers, safe routes, and credited route
covers pass review.

DoBIH `2390`, Pillar Rock, stays blocked from summit-route publication in both
lists. The LDWA accepts claims that omit this named technical climb. Production
must model that exact member exception, not a rule that lets any member be
omitted.

The 45-of-83 figure describes the ready source stack only. It does not show
production coverage and does not prove a worldwide share. The JSON amendment
pins the v1.6 hashes, stack base, fixture hash, roster hashes, list IDs,
identity counts, close-pair count, and safety rule.
